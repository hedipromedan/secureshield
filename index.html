About the project

Secure Shield is a project run by three communities, creating 3 short documentaries about online child sexual exploitation and abuse (OCSEA).

Implemented by

  • Digital Safety Taskforce
  • Hero of Digital Protection
  • GAS Mataram

Supported by

  • Pusat Kajian dan Perlindungan Anak
  • Kindernothilfe
  • German Cooperation (Deutsche Zusammenarbeit)

These films deal with a sensitive topic. Viewer discretion is advised.
